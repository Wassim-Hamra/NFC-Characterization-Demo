ILLUSTRATIVE PARAMETER LIMITS TABLE

Parameter: Carrier Frequency
Symbol: f_carrier
Unit: MHz
Nominal: 13.56
LSL (Lower Spec Limit): 13.50
USL (Upper Spec Limit): 13.62
Test Condition: Room temperature, nominal supply voltage
Notes: Deviation from 13.56 MHz causes coupling efficiency loss and potential decode errors

Parameter: Load Modulation Amplitude
Symbol: V_LM
Unit: mV
Nominal: 160
LSL: 120
USL: 200
Test Condition: Standard coupling distance, nominal field strength
Notes: Below LSL the reader cannot reliably detect the uplink signal

Parameter: Receiver Sensitivity
Symbol: S_rx
Unit: dBm
Nominal: -60
LSL: -65
USL: -50
Test Condition: Reference antenna at standard distance
Notes: More negative = better sensitivity; USL violation means degraded range performance

Parameter: Modulation Depth
Symbol: m_ASK
Unit: %
Nominal: 10
LSL: 7
USL: 14
Test Condition: Standard test field, nominal modulation index command
Notes: ISO 14443 specifies the modulation index range for reliable decoding

─────────────────────────────────────────────
HOW TO READ SPECIFICATION LIMITS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
LSL (Lower Specification Limit): The minimum acceptable measurement value. A measurement below the LSL is out of specification.

USL (Upper Specification Limit): The maximum acceptable measurement value. A measurement above the USL is out of specification.

Nominal: The target or ideal value. The process should aim to produce measurements close to the nominal with minimal spread.

Pass/Fail rule: A measurement passes if LSL ≤ measured_value ≤ USL. This is a deterministic binary decision based on measured data and the defined limits. It is not a statistical estimate.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
PROCESS CAPABILITY INDICES
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Cp = (USL - LSL) / (6σ)
  Measures the potential capability of the process if it were perfectly centred.
  Cp ≥ 1.33 is typically required for production release.

Cpk = min( (USL - μ)/(3σ), (μ - LSL)/(3σ) )
  Measures the actual capability, accounting for centring.
  A low Cpk relative to Cp indicates the process mean is shifted toward one limit.

A systematic shift in a lot can reduce Cpk significantly even when Cp remains acceptable, because the mean has moved toward one of the specification limits.
